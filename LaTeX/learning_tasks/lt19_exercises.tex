\enquestion{11.1}{
    An article in \textit{Concrete Research} [\enquote{Near Surface Characteristics of Concrete: Intrinsic Permeability} (1989, Vol. 41)] presented data on compressive strength $x$ and intrinsic permeability $y$ of various concrete mixes and cures.
    Summary quantities are $n = 14$, $\sum y_{i} = 572$, $\sum y_{i}^{2} = 23,530$, $\sum x_{i} = 43$, $\sum x_{i}^{2} = 157.42$, and $\sum x_{i} y_{i} = 1697.80$.
    Assume that the two variables are related according to the simple linear regression model.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Calculate the least squares estimates of the slope and intercept.
        Estimate $\sigma^{2}$.
        Graph the regression line.
        \item Use the equation of the fitted line to predict what permeability would be observed when the compressive strength is $x = 4.5$.
        \item Give a point estimate of the mean permeability when compressive strength is $x = 3.3$.
        \item Suppose that the observed value of permeability at $x = 3.3$ is $y = 45.6$.
        Calculate the value of the corresponding residual.
    \end{enumerate}
}
