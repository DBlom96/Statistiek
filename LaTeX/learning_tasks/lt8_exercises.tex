\enquestion{5.49}{

    It is known that $X$ is normally distributed with a mean of $0.1$ millimeter and a standard deviation of $0.00031$ millimeter,
    and $Y$ is normally distributed with a mean of $0.23$ millimeter and a standard deviation of $0.00017$ millimeter.
    The value of $\rho$ of these variables is equal to $0$.
    Specifications call for a lamp to have a thickness of the ink corresponding to $X$ in the range of $0.099535$ and $0.100465$ millimeter and $Y$ in the range of $0.22966$ and $0.23034$ millimeter.
    What is the probability that a randomly selected lamp will conform to specifications?
