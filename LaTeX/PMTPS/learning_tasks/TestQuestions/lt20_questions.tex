A pub owner tries to estimate how many barrels of beer ($B$) go through during and after an important football game. 
He suspects that the number of barrels sold is related to the temperature ($T$) in the pub.
For seven such nights it is known how high the temperature was.

The data is as follows:
\[
 \sum b = 138, \quad  \sum b^2 = 2776, \quad \sum t = 153, \quad \sum t^2 = 3371, \quad \sum b \cdot t = 3044.
\]
Estimate the correlation coefficient.
Is the correlation significant?
