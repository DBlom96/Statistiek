Suppose that $X$ has a normal distribution with unknown mean $\mu$ and unknown standard deviation $\sigma$.
A random sample of $n = 5$ is selected from this population:
\[
    x_1 = 91.6, \quad x_2 = 87.3, \quad x_3 = 90.8, \quad x_4 = 89.6, \quad x_5 = 91.2
\]
Find --- without using the option TESTS of the graphical calculator --- a \SI{95}{\percent} two-sided confidence interval for the mean $\mu$.
