Suppose that $X$ has a normal distribution with unknown mean and unknown standard deviation.
A random sample of $n = 16$ is selected from this population.
The sample mean is $38$ and the sample variance is $9$.
Determine --- without using the option TESTS of the graphical calculator --- the $p$-value of the test $H_0: \mu = 36.7$ versus $H_1: \mu \neq 36.7$.
